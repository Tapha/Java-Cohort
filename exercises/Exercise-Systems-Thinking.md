### 1. Content → Emergence Bridge

Choose one unexpected type of content you keep seeing online.

Unexpected type of content: day in the life of a fast food worker

Answer:

* What is the content?
People film themselves working completing orders through their phone, other people filming or 1st person view camera. It shows the tasks they do day to day to do their job. 
  
* Where are you seeing it?
I am seeing it across all social media platforms from TikTok to Instagram reels. 
  
* Why does it feel unexpected or oddly common?
It has always been rare for people to film themselves doing their job. It has always been seen that people should concentrate on doing their job rather than making content while being paid. 
  
* What larger behaviour, trend, anxiety, desire, or social shift might it be connected to?
I think this is linked to people's enhanced curiosity for what other people do in their lives which has only increased exponentially during the rise of social media. 
  
* What is the hidden link between the content and that wider emergence?
If people are making videos giving a greater insight into what daily life and reality is like for them, this creates a lot of interest and curiosity for people. The curiosity is drawn from the fact that everyone's lives are different so to get a greater insight into someone else's work day, we find that very interesting. 
  
* Why might this content be appearing more now?
TikTok has allowed quick video production which means that people no longer need to make and produce YouTube long form videos which are time intensive. TikTok has allowed people to create videos on their phone very conveniently, which means people can do it at their workplace.
