## Systems Thinking Questions 🧠

---

### 1. Content → Emergence Bridge

Choose one unexpected type of content you keep seeing online.

Answer:

* What is the content?
* Where are you seeing it?
* Why does it feel unexpected or oddly common?
* What larger behaviour, trend, anxiety, desire, or social shift might it be connected to?
* What is the hidden link between the content and that wider emergence?
* Why might this content be appearing more now?

5. Since we live in an attention economy, being able to get said attention is linked to being able to make more money

### 2. Distance Ladder

Choose one content pattern.

Now link it across increasing distance:

* What is the obvious link?
* What is a slightly wider cultural link?
* What is a social or economic link?
* What is a psychological link?
* What is the strangest but still believable link?
* Which link is the strongest, and why?

Example starting point:
“People filming their desk setups.”

```text
1. People filming themselves talking about their personal/questionable/craziest stories
2. People are becoming more open as with the growth of the internet more niches have been made more accessebile/known about by the public and so people are more comfortable expressing more
3. People are looking more for people like them online
4. People feel like they may be more seen if they manage to find people who understand their experiences online
5. Increasing lonliness leads to more embarrsing content stories
6. the psychological link
```

### 3. Mechanism Test

Pick one strange connection.

Example:
“Skincare routines are connected to financial anxiety.”

Answer:

* What exactly is the connection?
* What pressure or need is driving both things?
* What behaviour repeats across both?
* What system is encouraging this behaviour?
* What evidence would make your link stronger?
* What evidence would weaken your link?

---

### 4. Weak Link → Strong Link

Rewrite each weak connection into a stronger systems insight:

* “AI is like magic.”
* “Football tactics are like chess.”
* “Crypto is like religion.”
* “Gym content is capitalism.”
* “TikTok trends are like viruses.”

For each one, answer:

* What is weak or vague about the original comparison?
* What shared mechanism can make it stronger?
* What is the improved version?
* What does the improved version help us understand?

---

### 5. Pattern → Prediction

Choose one content pattern you keep seeing.

Answer:

* What is the content pattern?
* What wider emergence does it point to?
* What mechanism connects them?
* What are 3 things you predict will happen next because of this pattern?
* Who could use this insight: a business, creator, teacher, investor, designer, or policymaker?
* What should they do differently because of it?

### Final Reflection

* Which connection had the greatest category distance?
* Which connection had the strongest mechanism?
* Which connection produced the most useful prediction?
* Did your best answer come from a wild leap or a precise structure?
* What did this exercise teach you about systems thinking?
