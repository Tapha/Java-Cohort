Produce a Founding Spec with exactly these sections:

1. PRODUCT TRUTH — one sentence. What this is, for whom, doing what. If you
   can't say it in one sentence the notes haven't decided yet; say so.
2. THE USER — who specifically. Not a demographic, a situation. The moment
   they reach for this.
3. THE CORE LOOP — the one repeated action that makes the product alive.
   Everything else is scaffolding around this loop.
4. WHY NOW — what changed in the world that makes this buildable/wanted today.
5. SUCCESS CRITERIA — 3–5 observable signals that it's working. Behavioural,
   not vanity.
6. NON-GOALS — what this explicitly is NOT and will not become in v1. Be
   ruthless; this section protects every document below.
7. LOAD-BEARING ASSUMPTIONS — the beliefs the whole thing rests on, each
   tagged [STATED] (in the notes) or [INFERRED] (you supplied it). Mark the
   single assumption that, if false, kills the product.
8. OPEN QUESTIONS — what the notes left genuinely undecided.

Rules:
- Separate what the notes SAY from what you INFER. Never launder an inference
  into a fact.
- Prefer one sharp claim over three hedged ones. The spec's job is to commit.
- Length is not a virtue here. A tight one-pager beats a vague three-pager.
